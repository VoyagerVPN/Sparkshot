## 📸 Capture

✓ **Fixed** — The selection frame follows the pointer more closely when you first drag or resize it after opening a capture.

✓ **Fixed** — Launching Sparkshot again keeps your current selection and annotations open, even if you start several copies at once.

## ✏️ Editor

✦ **New** — Hold Alt to drag a text annotation while editing it.

✓ **Fixed** — Copied screenshots now include a PNG for apps that previously could not paste them as images.

## ⚙️ Tray and settings

↻ **Changed** — The tray menu now uses standard Windows controls and closes with Esc or a click outside.

↻ **Changed** — The settings window reopens in the same place after you copy, save, or cancel a capture started there.

✦ **New** — Choose English or Russian in Settings.

**Upgrading from 0.1.8?** Choose Exit from the tray menu, replace Sparkshot.exe in its current folder, and launch it again to keep your settings.
